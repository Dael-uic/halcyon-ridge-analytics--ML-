Company profile

Mission

Give commanders and field medics clear, evidence-based forecasts from combat operation data, so decisions on staffing, evacuation, and planning are made with facts rather than instinct.

Vision

A future where every after-action report feeds a learning system that lowers preventable casualties and gets the right care to the right person first.

Operational goals

Cut forecasting error on casualty planning, shorten triage time, flag mass-casualty risk before an operation starts, and keep every model explainable to non-technical staff.

Background

Founded in 2016 by four data scientists and a former field-medical officer, Halcyon Ridge Analytics Corp. (fictional, headquartered in Quezon City) began by digitizing paper after-action reports. Today it supports defense ministries and humanitarian peacekeeping missions with casualty analytics, medical logistics planning, and triage decision support.

The data behind the models

Two generated datasets feed every model. Each model below trains in your browser when this page loads, on 80% of the rows, and is scored on the 20% it has never seen.

operations.csv: 1,000 combat operations

One row per operation: terrain, weather, time of day, duration, force size, enemy-to-friendly ratio, air support, intelligence quality, armor share, medevac time, casualty rate, outcome, incident type, and a mass-casualty flag.

casualty_triage.csv: 1,200 casualty cases

One row per injured soldier: age, heart rate, blood pressure, breathing rate, consciousness score (GCS), bleeding level, evacuation delay, assigned triage category, and survival.
